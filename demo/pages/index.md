# FlexSearch for Cecil

This website is the demo of the [_FlexSearch_ component theme](https://github.com/Cecilapp/theme-flexsearch) for [Cecil](https://cecil.app).

Click the **Search** button in the header, or press `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `K`, then try a few queries:

- `install` or `composer` to find the installation guide
- `dark mode` or `custom properties` to find the styles
- `release` to find the blog posts (displayed with their date)
- `serch` (sic): fuzzy matching kicks in when nothing matches strictly

Results are grouped by section (_Documentation_ and _Blog_), and each documentation page is split on its headings, so a result links straight to the right anchor.
